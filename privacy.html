Privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy covers the Google account connection named Codex & Hermes Personal PC. It provides Gmail metadata summaries and a primary Google Calendar agenda through a personal Windows assistant.

Information accessed

After you authorize the connection, its current Google helpers can retrieve:

  • Gmail: subjects, sender details, dates, and message or conversation identifiers for a bounded search result page. They do not retrieve message bodies or attachments.
  • Primary Google Calendar: event identifiers, titles, start and end times, locations, status, and attendee counts. The helpers omit event descriptions and attendee addresses from their results.
  • Account identity: basic profile and email information used by the Google authorization connection, plus tokens that permit the authorized API requests.

The requested Gmail and Calendar permissions are read-only. Those Google permissions are broader than the fields the current helpers retrieve; metadata-only and primary-calendar-only access are limits of these helpers.

Use and AI processing

The connection uses Google information to answer your requests for email metadata summaries, message discovery, and calendar assistance. Relevant information and text derived from it may be included in the assistant conversation.

When your selected model or AI service processes that request, the information needed for the assistance may be processed locally or sent to the selected provider's servers. The provider's retention, human-access practices, and processing settings depend on the specific service and account configuration. Google's Limited Use restrictions continue to apply to that processing.

The connection is intended to share Google information only as necessary for the assistance you request. It is not intended to publish that information to other users or unrelated services.

Storage and protection

Google connection credentials are encrypted using the Windows keyring. Client configuration and the credential store are kept in a private folder for your Windows account, with restricted local permissions. Google passwords are entered in Google's sign-in flow, not on this website.

Retrieved information, prompts, and derived answers may remain in assistant chat history, saved notes, local backups, or the selected provider's records, depending on how those systems are configured. Credential protection does not mean every conversation or saved answer is encrypted by this connection.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can revoke access in your Google Account connections. You can also remove the local connection credentials and client configuration from the private Windows setup.

Revoking access stops future authorized Google reads. It does not automatically erase existing chat history, notes, backups, or provider-held records. Delete those copies through the relevant assistant, storage, or provider controls. This connection does not set one retention period for all of those systems.

Google authorization can expire or be revoked. You may need to sign in and consent again to resume access.

Google API Limited Use

Codex & Hermes Personal PC commits to handling Google API information, including transfers to other services, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements.

These requirements limit use to the disclosed features and restrict transfers, human access, and uses of derived information. The Google Workspace user data and developer policy also prohibits using Google Workspace data for advertising, resale, or training or improving general-purpose AI models.

Before Google information is sent to a selected AI service, that service's terms and settings must support these restrictions. This page does not certify a provider's training or retention settings.

This website and privacy questions

These informational pages contain no analytics code, tracking scripts, forms, or account sign-in. GitHub Pages hosting records visitors' IP addresses for security, including visits made without signing in to GitHub. See GitHub's Pages data-collection disclosure and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

For privacy questions, use the app support contact shown on Google's consent screen for Codex & Hermes Personal PC. The operator should update this policy and obtain any required consent before changing the disclosed use of Google information.
