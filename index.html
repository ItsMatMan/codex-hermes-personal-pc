About Privacy

Google account assistance, from your Windows PC.

Codex & Hermes Personal PC connects a personal Windows assistant to Gmail and Google Calendar so it can help with selected email details and an agenda when you ask.

What the connection does

  • Gmail metadata summaries: find a bounded set of messages and summarize subjects, senders, and dates. The current helpers do not retrieve message bodies or attachments.
  • A primary-calendar agenda: read upcoming event titles, times, locations, status, and attendee counts for a selected date range.

The Google helpers described here use read-only access. They do not send email, mark messages as read, or create, change, or delete calendar events.

How assistance works

You authorize your Google account through Google's sign-in and consent flow. The connection runs on your Windows PC, using the private assistant configuration.

Relevant Google information may be included in the assistant conversation and processed by the model or AI service you select. Processing can occur locally or through that provider's servers, depending on your configuration.

This website explains the connection. It does not host the assistant or provide a Google sign-in form.

Your data and choices

The privacy policy describes the information accessed, local credential storage, AI processing, and ways to disconnect or remove saved data.

You can review or remove the connection in your Google Account. Authorization can expire or be revoked, and reconnection may be required.
